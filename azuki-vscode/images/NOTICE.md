# Icon artwork

`icon.png` is adapted from copyrighted artwork from the video game _Enbito_
(2023), using the Echo walking-pose study in Azuki's `mascots_playground/`.

`azuki-file-light.svg` and `azuki-file-dark.svg` are the adopted Bravo
(bean seam) vector study from the same playground.
